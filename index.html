Study the law that decides who sees your data.

DPGSA is the Data Privacy and Governance Students’ Association at the University of Nairobi. We meet, moot, write and argue about privacy, cybercrime and the rules that run the internet.

Our mission

To nurture the next generation of experts in technology and innovation law.

We bring together students who want to understand data privacy, governance, and how law and technology shape each other.

  • Education & awareness

    Trainings, workshops, talks and webinars on data privacy law.

  • Research & writing

    The essay-writing competition, publications and a shared knowledge base.

  • Community & careers

    Peer connections and career support for students heading into law and tech.
