Where data, rights and technology meet.

DPGSA brings University of Nairobi students together around data privacy, governance, digital rights and emerging technology.

Who we are

Curious minds. Real-world questions.

The Association is a space for students to learn about data privacy, governance and digital rights, and to work out what fast-moving technology means for ordinary people.

  • Learn

    Trainings, talks and a shared knowledge base.

  • Practise

    Moots, debates and the essay-writing competition.

  • Connect

    Peers, practitioners and career support.

What drives us

Mission and vision

Mission

To develop the next generation of practitioners and experts in technology and innovation law.

Vision

A global network of young data privacy and governance enthusiasts, with information, education, peer connections and career support.

Our community

Part of a national community

DPGSA is the university student wing of the Data Privacy and Governance Society of Kenya (DPGSK).

The student wing of DPGSK. DPGSA is the affiliated university student wing of the Data Privacy and Governance Society of Kenya (DPGSK). Together, DPGSK and DPGSA form Kenya’s primary professional and academic community for data protection compliance, ethical data handling and digital governance.
